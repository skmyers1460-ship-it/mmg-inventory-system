# MMG Inc. Inventory Management System

## Internal Inventory Management Application

The MMG Inc. Inventory Management System is an internal business application designed to support inventory management and operational planning for MMG Inc.

The application integrates with QuickBooks Online to support authorized internal business functions including:

- Inventory monitoring and reconciliation
- Sales and demand analysis
- Purchasing analysis
- Supplier and vendor management
- Inventory forecasting
- Reorder calculations and recommendations
- Inventory reporting and operational planning

## Authorized Use

This application is intended solely for authorized MMG Inc. personnel. It is not offered as a public or consumer-facing software service.

## QuickBooks Online Integration

The application may connect to MMG Inc.'s QuickBooks Online account through Intuit's authorization services.

Access to QuickBooks Online information is limited to authorized business purposes associated with MMG Inc.'s internal inventory operations.
