# Disconnect from QuickBooks Online

## MMG Inc. Inventory Management System

This page provides disconnection information for MMG Inc.'s internal inventory management application.

## Disconnecting the Application

Access to the QuickBooks Online integration is restricted to personnel authorized by MMG Inc.

An authorized MMG Inc. user may disconnect or revoke the inventory application's access to QuickBooks Online through the applicable Intuit or QuickBooks Online account controls.

Disconnecting the application prevents it from obtaining new QuickBooks Online information through the disconnected authorization.

## After Disconnection

Disconnecting QuickBooks Online does not automatically alter or delete MMG Inc.'s underlying QuickBooks Online accounting records.

Information previously obtained or generated for legitimate MMG Inc. business purposes may remain subject to MMG Inc.'s applicable business, legal, accounting, and data-retention requirements.

If access is required again, an authorized MMG Inc. user must complete the approved QuickBooks Online connection or reconnection process.

## Support

Questions regarding disconnection of the MMG Inc. Inventory Management System should be directed to MMG Inc.'s designated business or system administrator.
