# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 29, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

MMG Inc. ("MMG," "we," "us," or "our") operates an internal inventory management application (the "Application") used by authorized MMG Inc. personnel.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is accessed, used, and processed by the Application, including information obtained through its connection to QuickBooks Online.

The Application is intended solely for MMG Inc.'s internal business operations and is not offered as a public or consumer-facing service.

## 2. Information Accessed by the Application

When an authorized user connects the Application to QuickBooks Online, the Application may access business information necessary for inventory management and related internal operations.

Depending on the functions being used, this information may include:

- Product and item information
- Inventory-related information
- Vendor and supplier information
- Customer information associated with business transactions
- Invoices
- Sales receipts
- Credit memos and returns
- Purchase orders
- Transaction dates, quantities, and related transaction information

The Application accesses QuickBooks Online information only through an authorized connection to MMG Inc.'s QuickBooks Online account.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Application may be used for MMG Inc.'s internal business purposes, including:

- Inventory monitoring and management
- Analysis of product demand
- Review of sales and return activity
- Review of purchasing activity
- Supplier and vendor management
- Inventory forecasting
- Reorder calculations and recommendations
- Inventory reporting and operational planning
- Reconciliation of inventory records with physical inventory counts

Information accessed through QuickBooks Online is not used to provide a public consumer service.

## 4. Access to the Application

The Application is intended for use only by MMG Inc. personnel or other individuals specifically authorized by MMG Inc.

Access to the Application and its QuickBooks Online connection is not intended to be made publicly available.

## 5. QuickBooks Online Integration

The Application integrates with QuickBooks Online to obtain business information authorized by MMG Inc.

Authorization to QuickBooks Online is provided through Intuit's authorization services. The Application is designed to request access only for purposes associated with MMG Inc.'s internal business operations.

Users may disconnect the Application from QuickBooks Online through applicable QuickBooks or Intuit account controls or through the Application's designated disconnection process.

## 6. Information Sharing

Information accessed by the Application is intended for MMG Inc.'s internal business use.

MMG Inc. does not use the Application to sell QuickBooks Online information to third parties.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary for legitimate business, technical, security, or compliance purposes.

## 7. Data Security

MMG Inc. takes reasonable measures appropriate to the Application's internal business purpose to protect information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention

Information processed by the Application may be retained as necessary to support MMG Inc.'s inventory management, accounting-related operations, business records, and other legitimate business purposes.

Retention may also depend on the source of the information, applicable business requirements, and legal or regulatory obligations.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MMG Inc. may update this Privacy Policy when the Application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Updates will be reflected on this page, including an updated effective date when appropriate.

## 10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Application's handling of information may be directed to MMG Inc. through its designated business contact.
