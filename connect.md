# Connect to QuickBooks Online

## MMG Inc. Inventory Management System

This page provides connection information for MMG Inc.'s internal inventory management application.

The application integrates with QuickBooks Online to support authorized internal business functions, including inventory management, sales-demand analysis, purchasing analysis, supplier management, forecasting, and inventory replenishment planning.

## Connecting or Reconnecting

Access to the QuickBooks Online integration is restricted to personnel authorized by MMG Inc.

An authorized MMG Inc. user must authenticate through Intuit's authorization process before the inventory application can access the company's QuickBooks Online information.

The application does not request users to provide their QuickBooks Online password directly to the inventory application.

If the QuickBooks Online connection expires, is revoked, or otherwise requires reauthorization, an authorized MMG Inc. user may initiate the approved reconnection process.

## Data Access

The application may access business information authorized through the QuickBooks Online connection as described in the MMG Inc. Inventory Management System Privacy Policy.

## Support

Questions regarding connection or reconnection of the MMG Inc. Inventory Management System should be directed to MMG Inc.'s designated business or system administrator.
